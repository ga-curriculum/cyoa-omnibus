## What the heck is this?

The field of data science contains many topics that don't fit our typical 2-hour lesson template. I still want to give you access to this knowledge, so I've allocated time in our calendar for you to watch these lessons pre-recorded. Please watch these lessons at your own pace, making sure to pause/unpause as you see fit. Lessons here fit two categories:

### Type I
Virtual lessons of this type are technical and might require a lot of pausing/unpausing. These lessons are either error-prone or tedious if you were following along on your own, but hopefully much more managable at your own pace.

- [Docker]()

### Type II
Virtual lessons of this type are short one-offs. They're cool, but don't lend themselves to longer lessons or even full breakfast hours. Snack-sized lessons!
